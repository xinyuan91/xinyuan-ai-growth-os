# Xinyuan Product System

## Business Model

Xinyuan builds AI-powered business growth systems for professional service entrepreneurs.

The product ladder follows:

Free Value → Low Ticket → Training Program → Private Coaching → Enterprise Solution

---

# Product Architecture

## 1. Entry Products

Purpose:

Build trust and identify potential clients.

Examples:

- AI growth workshops
- Positioning diagnosis
- Business assessments
- Experience sessions

---

## 2. Training Programs

Purpose:

Help practitioners build their business foundation.

Core topics:

- Positioning
- High-value product design
- Content systems
- Private domain conversion
- AI tools and automation

Example:

Psychological Healing Million Growth Program

---

## 3. Private Coaching

Purpose:

Provide customized business transformation.

Includes:

- Personal positioning
- Product strategy
- Content planning
- Sales optimization
- AI employee implementation
