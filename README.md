# Xinyuan AI Growth OS

An open-source AI growth operating system for coaches, creators and consultants.

## Overview

Xinyuan AI Growth OS is a practical AI framework designed to help service-based entrepreneurs build scalable businesses with AI.

It combines AI agents, knowledge systems, content workflows and business frameworks into one operating system.

## Why This Project

Many coaches, consultants and creators have valuable expertise but struggle with:

- Building a clear personal brand
- Creating consistent content
- Turning knowledge into products
- Managing client relationships
- Scaling their services

AI can become more than a productivity tool.

It can become a business partner.

## Core Modules

### AI Content Assistant

Helps transform ideas, experiences and expertise into:

- Social media content
- Articles
- Short videos
- Educational materials

### AI Case Study Assistant

Transforms client conversations and experiences into reusable knowledge assets.

### AI Sales Assistant

Supports customer conversations, needs analysis and service design.

### AI Knowledge System

Organizes frameworks, experiences and business knowledge into an AI-powered workspace.

## Vision

Build an open AI ecosystem that helps more experts, creators and consultants amplify their impact.
