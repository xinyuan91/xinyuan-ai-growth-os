# AI Content Assistant Skill

## Overview

AI Content Assistant helps creators, coaches and consultants transform their expertise, experiences and client insights into valuable content.

It follows a structured workflow:

Input → Extract → Transform → Publish

---

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Experience Extraction

Transform raw experiences into:

- Key insights
- Lessons learned
- Stories
- Frameworks

### 2. Content Transformation

Generate content formats including:

- Social media posts
- Articles
- Short video scripts
- Educational materials

### 3. Personal Brand Alignment

Ensure every piece of content reflects:

- Personal expertise
- Unique perspective
- Brand positioning
- Audience needs

---

## Basic Workflow

1. Input your experience, case study or idea.

2. AI analyzes:
   - Core value
   - Target audience
   - Content angle

3. AI generates:
   - Content structure
   - Draft version
   - Optimization suggestions

---

## Philosophy

AI should not replace human expertise.

AI should amplify valuable knowledge and help experts share their impact with more people.
