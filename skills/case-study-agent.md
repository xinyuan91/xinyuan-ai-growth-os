# AI Case Study Agent Skill

## Overview

AI Case Study Agent helps coaches, consultants and service professionals transform client experiences into structured knowledge assets.

A single client interaction can become:

Client Experience → Case Study → Content Assets → Brand Trust

---

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Case Extraction

Analyze client conversations and experiences to identify:

- Client background
- Original challenges
- Key turning points
- Transformation results
- Valuable insights

---

### 2. Knowledge Structuring

Transform raw experiences into:

- Case studies
- Teaching materials
- Frameworks
- Success stories

---

### 3. Multi-platform Content Generation

Convert one case into different formats:

- Social media posts
- Articles
- Short video scripts
- Educational content

---

## Workflow

1. Input client experience or consultation notes.

2. AI extracts:
   - Problems
   - Solutions
   - Transformation process
   - Core value

3. AI generates:
   - Structured case study
   - Content angles
   - Publishing suggestions

---

## Philosophy

Every valuable client experience is an asset.

AI helps experts preserve knowledge, amplify impact and build long-term trust.
